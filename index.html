Anime:P

Anime:P is an independent anime discovery interface. It does not host or re-upload anime episodes. Video playback is provided by third-party sources only when an authorized embed is configured and available.

Starter catalog entries are examples for the interface. Add official video IDs and verify regional availability before presenting an episode as playable.
